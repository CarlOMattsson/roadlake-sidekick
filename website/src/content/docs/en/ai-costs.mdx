  <section class="buyhow" aria-labelledby="h-keys">
    <h2 id="h-keys">A subscription does not cover everything</h2>
    <p>Anything your own server runs by itself needs an API key from Anthropic or OpenRouter, and the provider bills you per call. None of it is on by default. You turn each part on and supply the key yourself.</p>
    <ul class="list">
      <li><b>Anthropic API:</b> billed by Anthropic per token. Set a spending limit in the Anthropic Console.</li>
      <li><b>OpenRouter:</b> one key for many models from many providers. The price depends on the model and the provider you pick.</li>
      <li><b>A local model</b> on your own network, for example Ollama or LM Studio: nothing per call.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="buyhow" aria-labelledby="h-limits">
    <h2 id="h-limits">Limits that Sidekick sets</h2>
    <p>These defaults apply to AI steps that run on your server. You can change them in your server settings.</p>
    <ul class="list">
      <li>At most 50 USD per calendar month for AI steps that bill per call.</li>
      <li>At most 2 USD per task.</li>
      <li>At most 3 attempts at one step.</li>
      <li>At most 50 AI steps per day.</li>
      <li>Runs on a local model cost nothing and can be kept out of the money limits.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>The server works out the most a call can cost before it makes the call, and refuses it if it would pass a limit. Sidekick's figure is an estimate. The provider's invoice is the record, so also set a limit with the provider. Memory also has its own daily limits on how much text is sent.</p>
    <p><b>Automations</b> are off until you approve one. Your Claude or Claude Code runs them, so the run itself is covered by the subscription. The check model that can review their suggestions runs on your server instead, with an API key or a local model, and is off by default. Its calls count against the limits for automations: at most 1 USD per run, a separate cap of 15 USD per month inside the 50 USD cap, and at most 20 runs per day. With OpenRouter we recommend a cheap model from a trusted host.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="buyhow" aria-labelledby="h-or">
    <h2 id="h-or">Choose with care on OpenRouter</h2>
    <p>With OpenRouter you pick the provider and the model, so the cost and the privacy are yours to choose well.</p>
    <ul class="list">
      <li><b>Choose providers you trust.</b> Prefer one that runs the model outside China and says it does not store your prompts and does not train on them.</li>
      <li><b>Where a model runs matters more than where it was made.</b> Open models developed in China, such as DeepSeek, are fine when a trusted host outside China runs them, for example Google, Azure or AWS. What counts is where the model runs and whether that provider stores or trains on your data.</li>
      <li><b>Use cheap models for simple jobs.</b> A short summary does not need the largest model.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Prices change, so we list none here. See <a href="https://www.anthropic.com/pricing" rel="noopener">Anthropic's pricing</a> and <a href="https://openrouter.ai/models" rel="noopener">OpenRouter's models and prices</a>.</p>
