<Section title="Talk your day through with Claude">
  <p>The most effective way to use Sidekick is a conversation with Claude in voice mode while you walk, cycle or drive. Don't dictate commands: let Claude ask about your day and talk freely about whatever is on your mind. Ten minutes of conversation usually turns into a good number of tasks, journal entries and links, and clears your head of the things that keep going round in it.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Start with something like: &quot;Ask me how my day has been, and help me get on top of everything I need to do.&quot;</li>
    <li>Claude asks follow-up questions: who is involved, when it has to happen, what belongs together. Then it creates the tasks, journal entries and contacts in Sidekick and links them.</li>
    <li>With other connectors in Claude, it can also go through Slack, email and your calendar to add tasks you missed and book meetings.</li>
    <li>Only talk when it is safe, for example hands-free in the car.</li>
  </ul>
</Section>

<Section title="Claude suggests, you approve">
  <p>Anything that would change without you doing it yourself becomes a suggestion, and you decide with one tap in the app.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>AI steps:</strong> Claude can plan the steps of a task. You approve the plan, the steps run, and you review the result.</li>
    <li><strong>Automations:</strong> standing instructions that run at the pace you choose. An automation runs only once you have approved the exact version shown, and any change needs your approval again.</li>
    <li><strong>The suggestion inbox in Home:</strong> suggestions are split into safe groups, which you approve at most three at a time, and suggestions to review. Whatever you approved or dismissed, you can undo.</li>
    <li><strong>Ready to talk:</strong> a prepared conversation, such as a review of your day, waits in Home when it is due. You start it with Claude or skip it for today.</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="sec__more">Nothing is applied on its own unless you turn that on for an automation, with a separate confirmation step and fixed caps. Automations, the suggestion inbox and Ready to talk come with the next release.</p>
