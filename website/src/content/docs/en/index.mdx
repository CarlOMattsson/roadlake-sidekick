<Section title="Talk your day through with Claude">
  <p>The most effective way to use Sidekick is a conversation with Claude in voice mode while you walk, cycle or drive. Don't dictate commands: let Claude ask about your day and talk freely about whatever is on your mind. Ten minutes of conversation usually turns into a good number of tasks, journal entries and links, and clears your head of the things that keep going round in it.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Start with something like: &quot;Ask me how my day has been, and help me get on top of everything I need to do.&quot;</li>
    <li>Claude asks follow-up questions: who is involved, when it has to happen, what belongs together. Then it creates the tasks, journal entries and contacts in Sidekick and links them.</li>
    <li>With other connectors in Claude, it can also go through Slack, email and your calendar to add tasks you missed and book meetings.</li>
    <li>Only talk when it is safe, for example hands-free in the car.</li>
  </ul>
</Section>
