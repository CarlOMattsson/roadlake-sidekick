## Bugs and suggestions

Issues and feedback are very welcome, and they are read. Describe the problem or the idea in an issue: what you did,
what you expected and what happened. Do not put secrets or personal data in an issue.

Report vulnerabilities privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not as issues.

## Pull requests

Pull requests are welcome, but they can only be handled to a limited extent. They are reviewed at the maintainers'
own pace, and there is no promise about when you get an answer, or that a change is accepted. For anything larger than
a small fix, please open an issue first and describe the idea, so that neither of us spends time on a change that does
not fit.

Contributions require signing the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). When you open a pull request, CLA Assistant
asks you in the pull request to sign it, once; the pull request cannot be merged before that. The agreement lets the
licensor keep licensing the project, including under a license key and in the paid version.

Run the checks in the [development guide](docs/contribute/development.md) before you open the pull request. The history of this
public repository is never rewritten (no force-push), so forks and links keep working.

## Build and test locally

To run Sidekick or look at the code, follow [QUICKSTART.md](QUICKSTART.md). The checks, code style and generated files
are in the [development guide](docs/contribute/development.md); releases are in [Release a version](docs/maintainers/release.md). [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) lists the
architecture rules that apply to every change, and decisions referred to as `D-0xx` are in
[docs/decisions/log.md](docs/decisions/log.md).
